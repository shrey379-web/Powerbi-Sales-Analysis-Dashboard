# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales-related metrics, product costs, selling prices, order-level performance, and city-wise business distribution.


## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on exploring sales data through an interactive business intelligence dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard presents key business metrics and visual comparisons across cities, products, sales representatives, order IDs, and quantities. It helps users explore selling prices, product costs, and transaction-level patterns through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key sales and product-related metrics.
- Analyze selling prices across different cities.
- Compare product costs and selling prices by order ID.
- Explore product cost patterns across different quantities.
- Compare product distribution by sales representative.
- Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven business analysis.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Products:** 10
- **Total Quantity:** 100
- **Sum of Selling Price:** ₹34K

*These values are displayed in the provided dashboard. Confirm the aggregation and currency settings in Power BI before interpreting them as final business totals.*

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 💰 1. Sales & Product KPI Monitoring

- Displays key product and selling-price metrics.
- Uses gauge visuals to provide a quick overview of selected measures.
- Helps users monitor business indicators in one place.

### 🏙️ 2. City-Wise Selling Price Analysis

- Compares selling prices across Bhopal, Bengaluru, Mathura, Hyderabad, and Pune.
- Uses a doughnut chart to visualize each city's contribution.
- Supports geographical comparisons of sales-related metrics.

### 👥 3. Product Distribution by Sales Representative

- Analyzes product-cost distribution by representative.
- Includes names such as Gopal, Adwait, Chitra, Madhav, and Rishabh.
- Enables comparisons across the categories shown in the dashboard.

### 🧾 4. Order-Level Price and Cost Analysis

- Compares the sum of selling prices and product costs by Order ID.
- Uses a clustered or stacked column-style visual to inspect transaction-level patterns.
- Helps explore variations in costs and selling prices across orders.

### 📦 5. Product Cost by Quantity

- Visualizes product cost against quantity.
- Helps explore how recorded costs vary across quantity values.
- Supports further investigation of quantity-related patterns in the dataset.

### 🎛️ 6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- **City:** Bengaluru, Bhopal, Hyderabad, Mathura, and Pune
- **Order Date**
- **State**

These filters allow users to explore different data segments and compare the displayed metrics.
